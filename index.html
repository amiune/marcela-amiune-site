Human accountability

Human Accountability in an AI-Mediated World

As AI increasingly recommends, predicts, generates and decides, one of the most important leadership questions is not simply what machines can do.

It is what humans remain responsible for.

Responsible AI requires organizations to define where judgment, accountability, creativity, empathy and contextual understanding must remain human, and to design systems that strengthen rather than quietly replace those capabilities.
